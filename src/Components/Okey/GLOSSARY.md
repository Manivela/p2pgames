# Okey

The words of the okey table: the tiles, what's laid, how a game runs in rounds, and the seats around it. Turkish where players say it in Turkish.

## Tiles

**Indicator** (gösterge):
The tile turned face up at the deal; it names the okey and scores nothing.
_Avoid_: okeyTile (in prose)

**Okey**:
The wild tile: the indicator's colour, one rank up (13 → 1).
_Avoid_: joker, wildcard

**Fake okey** (sahte okey):
The tile that stands for the okey's own face value.

**Rack** (ıstaka):
A seat's two rows of slots; a gap between tiles separates groups.

**Middle pile**:
The face-down tiles not dealt, drawn from one at a time.
_Avoid_: deck, draw pile (in prose)

**Discard pile**:
The tiles a seat has thrown, the latest on top, beside the next seat.

## Melds

**Küt**:
Three or four tiles of one rank, each a different colour.
_Avoid_: set (in rules text), group

**Seri**:
Three or more tiles of one colour in a row of ranks; 12-13-1 only where runs wrap.
_Avoid_: run (in rules text), straight

**Çift**:
Two identical tiles, or the okey with any tile.
_Avoid_: double

**Meld** (per):
A küt, seri or çift laid on the table, owned by who laid it.

**Opening** (el açma):
A seat's first lay-down of a hand, as the round asks: set melds, enough points, or enough pairs.

**Elden**:
Finishing a hand from the rack without having laid anything.

## Rounds and hands

**Game**:
Everything from Start to the last round's scores, played by one ruleset.

**Ruleset**:
The settings a game is played by; classic, Americano and 101 are presets of it.
_Avoid_: mode, variant

**Round** (el, in the table's Turkish):
One of the ruleset's rounds, each with what it opens with; dealt again when nobody finishes it.

**Hand**:
A seat's tiles. In rules text also one deal of a round, played until someone finishes or the middle pile runs out — the two meanings share the word, as "el" does.

**Deal**:
Shuffling the tiles and handing out the racks that start a hand.

**Turn**:
A seat's go: draw, then lay or add if it can, then discard.

**Finish** (bitirmek):
Throwing your last tile to the middle, which ends the hand.

## Taking tiles

**Işler taş** (catch):
A discard that fit a meld on the table when thrown; anyone else may catch it, and the thrower pays.

**Claim**:
Taking the latest discard out of turn, where the ruleset allows it.

**Okey swap**:
Taking an okey off a table meld for the tile it stands for; one taken before opening must open the hand or go back.

## Table and seats

**Seat**:
A place at the table, numbered round it, sat in by a player, a bot, or nobody.
_Avoid_: slot (that's on the rack)

**Player**:
A person at the table; one browser is one player.
_Avoid_: user (in prose), client

**Bot**:
A seat played by the rules on a page at the table, making way for its player when they're back.

**Away**:
A seat whose player dropped out (a reload, a phone that slept); theirs to come back to, and still dealt.

**Stand up**:
Leaving a seat, its hand left for someone else to sit down to; out of the next deal unless someone does.

**Free a seat**:
Taking a seat out of the game; mid-round its tiles go back into the middle pile.

**Sit down mid-round**:
Taking an empty seat during a hand, dealt a rack from the middle pile.

**Partners** (eşli):
Four players in two pairs, each across the table from the other.
